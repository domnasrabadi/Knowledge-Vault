1. **Code** – turn instructions into scalable, repeatable work done by machines
    - Software tools and scripts
    - APIs and integrations
    - No-code automation
    - AI assistants and agents
    - Systems that reduce manual work
2. **Media** – turn content into reusable assets that generate attention over time
    - Writing (articles, books, newsletters)
    - Video (YouTube, courses, explainers)
    - Podcasts and interviews
    - Visuals, infographics, and frameworks
    - Personal brand or content flywheel
3. **Capital** – use money to accelerate growth, access, or efficiency
    - Buying systems or reach (ads, assets)
    - Allocating capital to unlock returns
    - Funding innovation or talent
    - Using capital as a strategic multiplier
4. **People** – multiply output by empowering others to solve, decide, and deliver
    - Hiring and building high-trust teams
    - Outsourcing and freelancing
    - Management and leadership leverage
    - Empowering operators and ownership
    - Culture as a scaling force
5. **Platforms** – tap into existing ecosystems for faster reach and scale
    - Social networks (e.g., LinkedIn, X)
    - Marketplaces (e.g., Amazon, Airbnb)
    - App stores or API ecosystems
    - SaaS platforms and distribution rails
    - Leveraging others’ infrastructure and scale
6. **Community** – activate aligned groups to co-create momentum and value
    - Online communities and member groups
    - Open-source contributors
    - Peer-led growth loops
    - Evangelists and superfans
    - Crowdsourced insight and content
7. **Systems** – build processes that reduce friction, errors, and reliance on ad hoc decisions
    - SOPs and checklists
    - Tool stacks and workflow automations
    - Decision-making frameworks
    - Dashboards and scoreboards
    - Scaling through clarity and structure
8. **Insight** – apply unique understanding to act earlier, smarter, or differently
    - Unique mental models
    - Industry or technical intuition
    - Pattern recognition from experience
    - Information arbitrage
    - Long-view strategic clarity
