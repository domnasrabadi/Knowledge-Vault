# 1 Core Philosophy
- **Be a Genius About the Things That Matter and Lazy About the Things That Don’t**
    - “Lazy Genius” balances intentionality with grace.
    - Instead of trying to do everything perfectly, focus effort on what actually matters to you.
- **No One-Size-Fits-All**
    - Reject rigid, universal systems; personalise approaches.
    - You define what matters in your current season of life, and that can change over time.
---
# 2 Mindset Overhaul
- **Progress, Not Perfection**
    - Stop chasing flawless routines or productivity hacks.
    - Focus on sustainability and doing the next right thing.
- **Permission to Pivot**
    - You’re allowed to change your mind or methods as needs shift.
    - Flexibility often beats strict discipline for long-term consistency.
---
# 3 The 13 Lazy Genius Principles
1. **Decide Once**
    - Eliminate decision fatigue by standardising recurring choices (e.g., meal planning, gifts, wardrobe).
2. **Start Small**
    - Begin with the smallest version of a habit or goal; incremental steps lead to lasting change.
3. **Ask the Magic Question**
    - “What can I do now to make life easier later?” guides daily actions with foresight.
4. **Live in the Season**
    - Accept current reality; don’t impose past or future ideals.
    - Adjust routines and expectations to fit the present season.
5. **Build the Right Routines**
    - Design routines around actual needs and energy levels.
    - Good routines support you—they don’t control you.
6. **Set House Rules**
    - Create gentle boundaries that reflect your values and simplify decisions.
    - Use rules to reduce conflict and clarify expectations for yourself and others.
7. **Put Everything in Its Place**
    - Organisation is about function, not perfection.
    - Identify stress points and build simple systems to support them.
8. **Let People In**
    - Don’t go it alone: share burdens, ask for help, and foster connection.
    - Vulnerability strengthens relationships and systems.
9. **Batch It**
    - Group similar tasks to reduce mental switching and increase efficiency.
    - Apply batching to errands, chores, creative work, or decisions.
10. **Essentialize**
    - Strip away the nonessential; not everything deserves your time and energy.
    - Choose what to care about and let the rest go without guilt.
11. **Go in the Right Order**
    - Don’t jump to solutions; understand the real problem first.
    - Ask: “What’s really not working here?” before acting.
12. **Schedule Rest**
    - Rest is a requirement, not a reward.
    - Proactively build rest into your life instead of waiting to burn out.
13. **Be Kind to Yourself**
    - Self-compassion fuels sustainable productivity.
    - Acknowledge limits, forgive mistakes, and reset when needed.
---
# 4 Practical Applications
- **Home**
    - Use _Decide Once_ for meal planning, batch chores, and create routines aligned with family rhythms.
- **Work**
    - _Essentialize_ tasks, batch deep work, and let rest plus clarity guide priorities.
- **Relationships**
    - Set gentle boundaries, practise presence, and allow imperfection.
- **Mental Load**
    - Reduce decision fatigue by building systems that reflect your values and current season.
---
# 5 Essence of the Lazy Genius Way
- You don’t have to do it all—just what matters.
- This is strategic effort, not laziness.
- Aligning actions with values makes life more manageable and meaningful.
