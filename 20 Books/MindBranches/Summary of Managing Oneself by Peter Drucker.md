## 0.1 Core Concept
- **Self‑management as the foundation of success:** long‑term achievement depends on knowing yourself and managing your strengths, values and environment effectively.
    In the modern knowledge economy, self‑awareness and adaptability are critical to remain effective and relevant.
## 0.2 Discovering Your Strengths
- **Feedback analysis:** record your decisions and compare the expected results with actual outcomes after 9–12 months to identify patterns of strengths and weaknesses.
- **Focus on strengths:** concentrate efforts where you can perform best rather than trying to fix every weakness. Leverage your strongest abilities to maximise contribution.
## 0.3 Understanding How You Work
- **Preferred learning style:** determine whether you learn best by reading, listening, writing or doing, and adapt your approach to fit your natural method.
- **Work style awareness:** know whether you work best alone or in a team, under stress or in a structured environment. Understand the conditions that enable your best performance.
## 0.4 Clarifying Your Values
- **Alignment of work and values:** your work must be consistent with your values to sustain motivation and integrity. Misalignment leads to dissatisfaction and poor performance.
- **Values in decision‑making:** use your values as a compass for making career and personal choices.
## 0.5 Knowing Where You Belong
- **Matching strengths, work style and values:** position yourself in roles and environments that align with all three; avoid forcing yourself into positions that conflict with your natural tendencies or ethical beliefs.
- **Deciding your contribution:** ask, _What can I contribute that will significantly impact my organisation or community?_ Contributions should align with your strengths, values and opportunities.
    Recognise that others also have unique strengths and values; adapt your approach to collaborate effectively.
## 0.6 Managing Relationships
- **Understanding others:** success requires working productively with others; this means understanding how they work, not just how you work.
- **Mutual dependence:** work productivity depends on recognising that others’ contributions are essential; success is shared.
## 0.7 The Second Half of Your Life
- **Reinvention and renewal:** plan for a “second career” or new focus later in life, whether in a different field, a non‑profit role or personal ventures. Continuous learning and exploration prepare you for transitions.
- **Avoiding decline:** without new challenges and goals, effectiveness and satisfaction diminish over time. Cultivate interests and networks beyond your current role.
## 0.8 Continuous Growth
- **Lifelong learning:** treat personal development as an ongoing process. Regularly reassess strengths, values and goals as circumstances change.
- **Self‑responsibility:** you are the CEO of your own career; no one else will manage your growth for you.
