1. **Protected Market Position** – legal, regulatory, or resource-based barriers that keep competitors out
    - Patents, trademarks, and exclusive IP rights
    - Government licenses, tariffs, or protected charters
    - Regulatory complexity favouring incumbents
    - Exclusive access to key resources (land, spectrum, raw materials)
    - Long-term or exclusive contracts (supply, distribution, customer)
2. **Unique Knowledge** – embedded expertise and insights that are difficult to replicate
    - Proprietary processes or trade secrets
    - Deep institutional knowledge and learning curves
    - Operational excellence built through experience
    - Customer or market insight unavailable to outsiders
3. **Scale and Cost Leadership** – cost or efficiency advantages tied to size or shared systems
    - Economies of scale (lower per-unit costs at volume)
    - Economies of scope (shared costs across products/services)
    - Fixed-cost leverage (spreading R&D, marketing, infrastructure)
    - Preferential terms from suppliers or partners
4. **Network and Platform Effects** – product/service becomes more valuable as more users join
    - Direct user network effects (e.g., social, messaging, gaming)
    - Two-sided platforms (buyers attract sellers and vice versa)
    - Ecosystem value from third-party developers, apps, or partners
    - Data feedback loops that improve service over time
5. **Switching Costs and Lock-In** – friction or loss that makes customers stay
    - Technical switching barriers (data migration, integrations)
    - Contractual or pricing lock-ins (subscriptions, volume deals)
    - Product bundling or suite dependency
    - Workflow or process integration that’s hard to replace
6. **Brand and Emotional Loyalty** – trust and attachment driving repeat business and pricing power
    - Strong brand equity and recognition
    - Perception of quality, reliability, or safety
    - Cultural or ethical alignment with customers
    - Emotional attachment or lifestyle fit
7. **Financial and Structural Advantage** – stronger funding, risk management, or strategic positioning
    - Access to low-cost capital or insurance float
    - Diversified, recurring, or resilient revenue streams
    - Capital efficiency and smart allocation
    - Structural control (e.g., ecosystem gatekeeper, exclusive channels)
8. **Adaptability and Innovation Capacity** – ability to evolve, pivot, or build the next edge
    - Consistent R&D investment and output
    - Organizational agility and modular design
    - Culture of experimentation and learning
    - Strategic capital reallocation to new opportunities
