Why I Enjoy ML More Than LLMs? Because every problem is not just prompts!  
  
I use LLMs every day. They’re powerful and useful.  
But, I enjoy working with traditional machine learning much more.  
  
Why?  
Math is at the center. With ML, I get to dive into equations, optimization and algorithms. It’s not just about asking the right prompt, it’s about proving, testing and understanding why something works.  
  
Tinkering is part of the process. ML invites you to adjust models, experiment with variants and get hands-on with the mechanics. It feels like solving a puzzle through mathematics.  
  
LLMs feel like app-building. Much of the work is about prompts, orchestration and user applications. That’s valuable, but for me, less exciting than algorithmic problem-solving.  
  
In short: LLMs make you a product builder. ML makes you a problem solver.
