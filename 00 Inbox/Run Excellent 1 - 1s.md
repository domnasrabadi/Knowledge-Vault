# Run Excellent 1:1s

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://pbs.twimg.com/profile_images/1458567254216425475/z9x5tQBv.jpg" width="220" />
</div>

- Most managers run their 1:1s wrong. They write the agenda. They ask the questions. They take 80% of the airtime
- The person owns the 1:1, not you. They come prepared with their wins, their metrics, their progress, their blockers, their lessons. You listen 90%. You speak 10%. You coach through questions, not solutions.

### The dashboard: 8 questions that show command.

- 1. How are you? (No really, how are you?)
- If the answer is real, it tells you whether to follow the rest of the dashboard or abandon it entirely.
- 2. What do you think we should focus on?
- The person sets the priority. They name what's taking up oxygen. You listen first, speak second.
- 3. How are you tracking against your goals?
- What changed since last week?
- They own the diagnosis before you layer in your view.
- 4. Are there notable wins or losses to discuss?
- 5. What problems are you focused on solving right now?
- What are they trying to get better at? Where do they spend attention when they're not in execution mode?
- 6. How are your people doing? (or: How are your key relationships?)
- If they manage, you're checking on their team. If they don't, you're asking about peers and partners.
- **7. How are you getting better?**
- What did they learn last month? How are they using that insight in this month's work?
